flowchart TD
    A[Welcome Page] --> B[User clicks 'Get Started' button]
    B --> C[showModalBottomSheet with Phone Number Input]
    C --> D[User enters phone number with country code]
    D --> E[User clicks 'Send Verification Code']
    E --> F[Immediately show PIN Modal - NO OTP]
    F --> G[User enters 6-digit PIN]
    G --> H[User clicks 'Sign In']
    H --> I[AuthCubit.signInWithPhoneAndPin called]
    I --> J[AuthService creates email from phone]
    J --> K[Try Firebase signInWithEmailAndPassword]
    K --> L{User Exists?}
    L -->|Yes| M[Sign In Successful]
    L -->|No| N[Create new Firebase user]
    N --> O[Create CustomerModel profile]
    O --> P[Sign In Successful]
    M --> Q[AuthState becomes AuthAuthenticated]
    P --> Q
    Q --> R[AuthWrapper detects authenticated state]
    R --> S[Navigate to Home Page]
    
    T[Error Handling] --> U[Show error message in PIN modal]
    K --> T
    N --> T
